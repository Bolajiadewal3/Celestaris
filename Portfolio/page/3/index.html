  • I – MUSINGS

    Nothing has form, violence and vitriol,
Nothing has shape, a void of mass caves,
Nothing was born from mere children of chaste,
Nothing sprung forth from gods and myth-makes.
    This nothing you speak of,
    Has nothing innate,
    How could it hold weight, or value to take, This 'byss that you speak of,
    Holds no such slight shape,
    'Tis empty, nothing - for nothing's sake
    This silence holds weight, do believe it so,
    The echoes of solace do hurt idled folk,
    The mired mage did profess its growth,
    Soon bear witness, from nothing, something grows,
    I believe these lies, not,
    I hold no such witch-worship,
    I believe in what's seen, what I hear bears unnerving,
    Then those eyes shall deceive,
    For their value is curtained,
    The mirror of learned life, is soon to be turgid.
    August 17, 2025

  • MAYHEM

    Of whom do you speak,
    When you shed rationales on mayhem, method and more,
    As if justifying the mire,
    Absolving the solvent for changing the mixture,
    Cleansing the blood still within demon agents.
    For what right was bestowed,
    To have you clean tarnished,
    I dare not respect such authority,
    I reject all that you muse.

    August 12, 2025

  • PIERCE

    If pain and pleasure be joint vice-virulence,
    I am both sick and satisfied,
    If naked tethers join junctions of jubilance,
    Deeper, dark leather ‘round souls leave dice.
    For ‘tis chance that such clamps be unbound and unwoven,
    Mere chance that the fiends show the airs of their souls,
    For both pain and pleasure are measured through eyes,
    That see smiles but not daggers in the palms of dark heathens.
    The blissful blisters mask the betrayal,
    One’s addiction to stress clouded star-aligned stabbings,
    Predictions of pestilence did appear on the brows,
    Yet the ignorance of pain-chase left the miser surrounded.
    Ides, or idle, misspelt demise,
    The wound bleeding pure with the glint of an angel,
    The soul sought suffering, welcomed want and thrill,
    And thus found itself sacrificed, to higher lord’s wills.
    If pain and pleasure be joint vice-virulence,
    You are both sick and satiated,
    If clothed creatures wish death on your sole desires,
    No weirgild can save you, your soul must retire.
    August 12, 2025

  • CRITIQUE

    Harsh on myself I admit I have been,
    For if I am the harshest, no pain is received,
    No love lost or blighted, just smiles unseen,
    Some tears, in the silence, I fear for me.

  • QUALIA

    Is my red, your red?
    Or my minute, yours?
    Is that flutter from my flame,
    Mirrored by your core?
    For that red I see is dangerous,
    Violent yet serene.
    Your red by your admission,
    Is life, perfect, clean.
    Could count a thousand more,
    Mere numbers void of meaning,
    Yet eyes of yours see digits,
    In shapes, words and feelings.
    Is my minute yours?
    This time we share, how so?
    My world does seem so fleeting,
    While yours, too slow and dull.
    August 12, 2025

  • BLINDED

    Blinded by passions,
    The yearns of the soul,
    I delegate prior memories to the pyre and pike,
    Prisms of witch welding,
    Lay siege to the whole,
    Kill carnal desires with brimstone and like,
    Culled crud from past battles,
    Soon seared in the flames,
    Yield space for new spires of golden delight,
    But a distant blur,
    An echo of hers,
    Don’t remember the babe, before you, mon cœur.

    August 12, 2025

  • REQUIEM FOR THE POLYMATH

     Derelict be the duplicity of the modern man,
    Where the math of the market differs so from that of the world,
    Where the demiurge deals in death through daemon,
    Not pestilence or just war.
    No need for epistles of glory,
    Condemnations or secret loves,
    No need for hearts to hit pages and etch scars on their scholars,
    What is this modern man?
    For whom words seem unruly.
    Assertions of credence mean more now than doubts,
    Inquisition tainted by the ’spanic shroud,
    No questions asked, no appearance pondered,
    Foul laws of the world stay hidden and harsh.
    Derelict be the duty of the modern man,
    Where the calling to action be unheard by perturbed,
    Soul fission flicks frailty upon human husks,
    Laymen meet root meaning, laymen do despair.
    No need for the angels to uphold havens,
    Ghosts and their stories untold by the tough,
    No need for the maker or made to taste tests,
    New laws do afflict ‘selves with stagnant self-wars.
    Assertions in darkness, no method from mad,
    Mere jests and crude jabs at the wisest of warring,
    Whom fight through the math, find features in filters,
    That man now lays dead, man of letters, withered.

    August 12, 2025
